## 🌟 What It Does

Vanilla Minecraft only shows dots for players on the locator bar. **Locator Tweaks** turns it into an all-in-one survival compass so you always know where you are and where your friends, base, and loot are.

![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/menu1.png)

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📍 Custom Waypoints ![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/waypoint_pin_0.png)
* **One-Click Pin (`B`):** Save your current spot instantly with a name and color.
* **Waypoints Menu (`U`):** View, search, toggle on/off, or edit all your pins.
* **Per-World Saving:** Singleplayer and server waypoints are kept completely separate.

![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/preview2.png)

---

### 💀 Automatic Survival Landmarks ![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/death_point_0.png)
* 💀 **Death Point:** Automatically marks where you died and shows how far away your dropped items are.
* 🛌 **Bed & World Spawn:** Always points back to your home bed or respawn anchor.
* 🌀 **Nether Portal Memory:** Remembers the portal you came through so you never get lost in the Nether.
* 🧭 **Lodestones:** Lodestone compasses show up directly on the bar.


---

### 👥 Player Heads on Your Compass ![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/alex.png)
* **Real Skin Faces:** Replaces plain colored dots with actual player heads. 
* **Player Manager (`J`):** Highlight friends with custom colors, resize icons, or hide players.
* **Glow Outlines:** Team colors make allies instant to spot.

---

### 🔍 Hold `Shift` for Instant Details (disabled by default)
Look toward any marker and hold **`Shift`** to see:
* **Distance & Height:** Distance in meters and whether it is above (`+dY`) or below (`-dY`) you.
* **Teammate Hearts:** Real-time health display of your friends.

![Locator Tweaks Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiader-45/Locator-Tweaks/main/githubassets/shifttooltip.gif)

---

### 🧭 Cardinal Directions & Clean HUD
* Clean **N, E, S, W** compass directions built into the bar.
* Flexible positioning: place the bar at the bottom or top of your screen.
* Optional **Compass-only mode**: show the bar only when holding a compass item.

## ⌨️ Controls

| Key | What it does |
| :---: | :--- |
| **`B`** | **Quick Waypoint** (save current spot) |
| **`U`** | **Waypoints Menu** (list, edit, toggle pins) |
| **`J`** | **Player Manager** (customize friend colors & heads) |
| **`Shift`** | **Inspect** (hold to reveal distance, height, and health) |

*(All keys can be changed anytime in Minecraft's Controls settings).*
